What is KaemKaar

Connecting Communities
Through Work

"Ais Karaan Kaem Aasaan."

We make work easy.

KaemKaar is a mobile-first marketplace that directly connects skilled workers and local hirers across J&K — eliminating exploitative middlemen, formalizing the informal economy, and creating fair, flexible income for Kashmir's youth.

  • 01
    No digital platform exists for local J&K workers. Opportunity is invisible, undiscoverable, and lost daily.
  • 02
    Middlemen pocket up to 30% of daily wages — a tax on the hardest workers in the valley.
  • 03
    Skilled workers go unseen. Without visibility, talent is wasted and daily earnings disappear.

Our Features

Built for Real Work,
Real People

Every feature is designed around the daily reality of J&K's workers and hirers — fast, simple, and trustworthy.

Hyperlocal Matching

Connect with verified workers and jobs within your own neighbourhood — within minutes, not days.

Instant Hiring

Post a job and get matched instantly. No calls to middlemen, no waiting — hire when you need it.

Verified Profiles

Trust built through transparent ratings, work history, and identity verification — on both sides.
